01 / Purpose

A site that explains itself.

The aim is not to collect notes for their own sake. It is to make a small body of knowledge useful enough to guide implementation, review, and future changes.

Two layers, two jobs

The site contains edited explanations, relationships, and current decisions. It should read as one coherent argument, not as a transcript of every conversation.

The archive preserves source files, rough notes, previous comparisons, and research material. It is a database of evidence and history, not the site's main navigation.

Working principles

  1. Preserve evidence before editing or reorganizing it.
  2. Promote only useful, supported material into the curated site.
  3. Mark uncertainty instead of silently turning it into fact.
  4. Link each decision to its evidence and each explanation to related topics.
  5. Test the published result, not just the source files.

What success looks like

A reader can start on any page, understand its role, follow links to the underlying evidence, and return to the larger structure without needing to remember a folder path.
